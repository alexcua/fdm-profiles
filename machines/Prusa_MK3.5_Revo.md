# Prusa MK3.5 — 0.6mm E3D Revo (Swappable)

**Nozzle:** E3D Revo swappable system — available sizes: 0.4mm, 0.6mm, 0.8mm  
**Slicer:** OrcaSlicer / PrusaSlicer
